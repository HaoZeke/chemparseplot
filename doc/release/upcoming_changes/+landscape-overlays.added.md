`plot_landscape_surface` can hide the dashed variance contours and fade cells far from the observations.
`show_variance_contours` defaults to true, and `fade_distance_above` defaults to no distance fade.
`plot_reduced_landscape` takes the same contour switch, plus `fade_distance` and `show_legend`.
`show_legend` defaults to true.
`plot_structure_strip` takes `label_fontsize` and `label_padding_px`.
`label_padding_px` is the caption inset from the bottom of the row, and a separate caption axis ignores it.
